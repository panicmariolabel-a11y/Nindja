🎓 Why This Matters

This tool demonstrates fundamental principles of probability theory:

1. House Edge: Roulette always favors the casino due to the 0 (and 00 in American roulette).
2. Independence: Each spin is completely independent. Previous results don't influence future spins.
3. Law of Large Numbers: Over many spins, results approach expected probability.
4. Gambler's Fallacy: The mistaken belief that past results change future probabilities.

Conclusion: No betting system can overcome the mathematical house edge. This simulator proves this principle mathematically.
